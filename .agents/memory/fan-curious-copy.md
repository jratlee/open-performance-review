---
name: Fan-curious copy
description: Copywriting direction for Is Harry Tired? fan-facing field records
---

The copy should sound like a Harry Styles fan asking a genuine question about a particular show, not like a brand manifesto, analyst dashboard, or abstract editorial concept. Use conversational questions, concrete concert details, and observations a fan would recognize. Keep the public-facing product playful and plain-language first; reserve technical terms for a short how-it-works explanation or internal data fields.

**Why:** Phrases such as “A night, kept plainly” felt authored by a design system instead of by someone interested in what happened at the show.

**How to apply:** Put warmth and curiosity in the hero, show-night framing, result labels, and observations. Explain evidence limits in everyday language such as “what we can see” and “no mind-reading,” while keeping the explicit no-diagnosis boundary available in the method copy.
