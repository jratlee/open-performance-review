---
name: Human field-record aesthetic
description: Visual direction for the Is Harry Tired? redesign
---

Prefer a restrained, practical field-record or venue/box-office artifact over a polished AI/editorial landing-page treatment. Use specific metadata, modest hierarchy, imperfect material details, and fewer decorative effects.

**Why:** The first ticket-led exploration felt too generic and AI-designed to the user, even though it used the requested physical-ticket metaphor.

**How to apply:** When exploring or integrating visual directions for this product, prioritize the record itself—entry number, date, venue, source signal, and filing language—over oversized slogans, gradients, and ornamental sections.
